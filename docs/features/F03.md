## Farmer stories and scope

1. For my owned field and existing crop season, I can record a sourced soil/crop profile, initial root-zone condition and actual irrigation events, then run a daily water balance from traceable weather inputs.
2. I can see reference ET, crop/actual ET, depletion, available water and an advisory net/gross irrigation depth and volume, with dates, units, assumptions and missing-data reasons.
3. I can inspect actual satellite-derived ET/biomass and water productivity only when source data, units, coverage and quality support the calculation; an unavailable product is shown as unavailable.

First slice: non-flooded upland crop water balance and advisory irrigation, not pump actuation. Include the pyWaPOR worker/API/status design in F03, but do not declare the full satellite-water-productivity scope complete until a genuine supported acquisition/product completes. Flooded paddy, salinity, waterlogging, managed groundwater and complex intercropping require supported models/parameters; do not quietly apply the upland model to them.

## Scientific requirements and primary research

Use short-grass reference ET for the selected FAO56 crop coefficients. pyfao56's reference function is ASCE standardized reference ET, with short/tall modes: record method/reference surface, and verify daily short-reference results against FAO examples rather than labelling every ASCE output identical to every FAO method. Inputs are elevation m, latitude degrees, radiation MJ/m²/day, temperature°C, vapour pressure kPa or valid humidity/dew-point inputs, windm/s with its measurement height. Require explicit humidity inputs for the initial slice; do not silently accept the library's inferred humidity fallback.

FAO56 separates reference ET, crop ET and water-stressed actual ET, and needs soil storage/root depth/initial condition and rainfall/irrigation/runoff/drainage accounting. Do not use ET0−rainfall as a complete irrigation prescription. Golden tests should use published numerical worked examples and daily balance closure, independently of wrapper implementation. [FAO56 Chapter8](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0e.htm), [Annex8 worked scheduling example](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0p.htm).

Use net irrigation at the root zone; gross application accounts for explicit efficiency exactly once. One mm over one hectare equals10m³; volume uses the owned field's server-side area. Validate finite inputs, 0<efficiency≤1, wilting point<field capacity, consistent volumetric units, meaningful root depths/stage dates and unique contiguous local dates. Missing rainfall or irrigation records are unknown unless the farmer explicitly confirms zero. Rainfall is not automatically100% effective; account for runoff/drainage and document assumptions about capillary rise. An assumed initial depletion is a labelled scenario, never a measured state.

Open-Meteo's hourly ET0/precipitation are previous-hour accumulations; daily ET0 is a daily sum. Build an explicit daily contract with units/timezone/completeness, and distinguish measured, reanalysis and forecast inputs. A forecast must not be saved as a historical measured observation. Provider-derived ET0 and locally calculated ET0 are separately identified. Commercial use requires appropriate service terms; do not reuse the free tier implicitly. [Weather units](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), [provider terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

Satellite productivity requires real same-period biomass or yield and actual water consumption: dry biomasskg/ha divided by ETamm×10 giveskg/m³. pyWaPOR biomass_prod iskgDM/ha/day; NPP isgC/m²/day and is not interchangeable with crop yield or dry biomass. Align periods/masks/valid area, integrate daily products correctly, reject zero/nonfinite denominators and return null/unavailable if either required product is absent. Satellite ET, crop-yield water productivity, biomass water productivity and applied-irrigation productivity must have separate names.

FAO currently describes global300m products and selected finer-resolution areas; Pakistani Khanewal/Sanghar20m examples are marked no longer produced. Validate the exact catalogue version, field/date coverage and usable pixels before choosing a product. Resampling coarse pixels does not yield a genuine field-resolution observation. Running pyWaPOR's own configured workflow is distinct from downloading FAO-produced products. [FAO WaPOR coverage/product definitions](https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/wapor-data/en/), [pyWaPOR documentation](https://www.fao.org/aquastat/py-wapor/).

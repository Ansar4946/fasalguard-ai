|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | High | Platform auth (hits F09/F10/F12 and every client) | `JwtAuthGuard` wraps the session lookup in a bare `catch` and answers `401 INVALID_ACCESS_TOKEN` for ANY failure, including database outage. Verified live: stopped `qa3-db`, `GET /auth/me` and `/farm-ledger` returned 401; the browser was redirected to `/login?returnTo=%2Falerts` with the `fg_access` cookie deleted.
